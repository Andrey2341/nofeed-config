Frequently asked

Is my Instagram password safe?

You sign in on Instagram's own page. NoFeed never sees or stores your password.

The feed or Reels appeared again

Instagram sometimes changes its website. Close and reopen the app to load the latest filters. If it keeps happening, email us.

Can I watch Reels my friends send me?

Yes. Reels sent in messages play normally; you just can't swipe to the next one.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › NoFeed › Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings.
